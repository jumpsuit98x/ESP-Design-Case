Final improvements

What changed in the last version

Issue found earlier Final improvement made Result
USB opening was too tight. The front cutout was widened and centered. USB-C cable connects without pushing the board.
Pin and wire space was limited. Side cutouts were added beside the pin rows. Jumper wires have more clearance.
The board could move during testing. Side supports were raised and small corner stops were added. The ESP32-C3 Zero sits more securely.
Prototype 2 used extra material. Unneeded wall thickness was reduced. The final print is lighter while staying strong.
Airflow under the board was weak. A raised base and vent gap were added. The board has more cooling space.

Final strengths

  • Secure board fit with better side support.
  • Clear access to the USB port and pin rows.
  • Smoother edges and cleaner presentation.

Remaining limitations

  • The holder is made for this specific ESP32-C3 Zero size.
  • Very thick cables may still need a larger front opening.
  • Future versions could include mounting screw holes.
