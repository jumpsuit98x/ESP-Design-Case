Prototype 1

First holder test

Prototype 1 tested the first 3D printed holder shape. The main goal was to check whether the ESP32-C3 Zero could sit securely without blocking the USB port or pins.

Pros

  • The board was mostly supported by the base.
  • The shape was simple and easy to print.
  • The first model helped confirm the holder size quickly.

Cons

  • The USB port opening was too tight for some cables.
  • The board could move slightly because the side walls were low.
  • Pin clearance was limited, especially when wires were attached.
  • The hinges were too short and did not give enough movement.
  • The top buttons could not be pressed properly while the part was fitted.
  • The design needed air holes, and the distance between parts needed adjusting.

Next steps

Planned improvements for Prototype 2

Widen the USB cutout

Make the front opening larger so different USB-C cable ends fit without rubbing.

Add pin clearance

Remove more material around the pin rows so jumper wires and solder points have space.

Raise the side supports

Increase wall height slightly to hold the board more securely without covering components.

Adjust hinges and spacing

Lengthen the hinges and change the distance between parts so the prototype opens and fits correctly.

Improve button and airflow access

Add top button clearance and air holes so the buttons can be pressed and heat can escape.
