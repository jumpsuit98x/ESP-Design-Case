Pros

  • The USB cable fit more easily through the larger opening.
  • The board was held more securely by the raised side supports.
  • Extra cutouts made wire routing easier.

Cons

  • The stronger walls used more filament than needed.
  • The corners were still a little sharp.
  • The airflow gap under the board could be improved.

Next steps

Planned improvements for the final design

Round the corners

Add small rounded corners so the holder feels smoother and looks more finished.

Add underside airflow

Create a small raised base and vent space to help air move under the board.

Reduce excess material

Keep the holder strong while trimming wall thickness where it is not needed.
